# Getting Started

1. Open the PCAP Streamlit application deployed for your laboratory or launch it locally.
2. Select the appropriate analysis tab.
3. Upload a compatible recording (such as ABF, AXGD, or AXGX), or upload a previously exported PCAP kinetics CSV in **Compare Groups**.
4. Inspect the results and confirm the detected AP/AHP landmarks against the waveform.
5. Export results as CSV when needed.

For local installation and execution, refer to the [repository README](https://github.com/theafairfax/pcap#readme) and its current dependency instructions.
